Service No. 01

Diagnostics & Check-Engine Light

A check-engine light can mean a loose gas cap or a failing sensor. Reading the code is only the first step. Good diagnosis means testing until the cause is confirmed, so you pay for the repair that actually fixes it.

What it covers

  • Reading and interpreting trouble codes
  • Testing sensors, wiring and connections
  • Drivability problems: stalling, hesitation, rough running
  • Electrical faults and warning lights

Signs you need it

  • Check-engine or other warning light is on
  • Engine runs rough, hesitates or stalls
  • Worse fuel economy than usual
  • A new noise, smell or vibration

What to expect: Describe what you notice and when it happens, like cold starts, highway speeds or braking. The more detail you share, the faster the cause can be pinned down.

Service No. 02

Wheel alignment on a Hofmann system

An alignment sets the angles of your wheels, known as camber, caster and toe, back to your vehicle maker’s specifications. Sixty Service uses a Hofmann alignment system that measures each wheel and shows the adjustments on screen.

What it covers

  • Measuring camber, caster and toe
  • Adjusting to manufacturer specifications
  • Centering the steering wheel
  • Checking tires for uneven wear

Signs you need it

  • Car pulls left or right
  • Steering wheel is off-center
  • Tires wearing on one edge
  • After new tires, a pothole or a curb hit

What to expect: alignments are a natural add-on to new tires or suspension work, and they help new tires wear evenly from day one.

Service No. 03

Brakes

Brakes are the most important safety system on your car. Squeals, grinding or a pedal that feels different are worth checking early, before worn pads damage rotors and a small job becomes a bigger one.

What it covers

  • Brake inspection
  • Pads and rotors
  • Calipers and hardware
  • Brake fluid and lines

Signs you need it

  • Squealing, squeaking or grinding when stopping
  • Soft, spongy or low brake pedal
  • Steering wheel or pedal vibration when braking
  • Car pulls to one side under braking

What to expect: Brake work starts with an inspection with the wheels off, so you know the condition of the pads, rotors and hardware before anything is replaced.

Service No. 04

Maintenance & Oil Service

Regular maintenance is the cheapest repair there is. Oil service and scheduled maintenance keep your engine protected and give the shop a chance to spot leaks, worn parts and other small problems early.

What it covers

  • Oil and filter service
  • Scheduled maintenance by mileage
  • Fluid checks
  • A look at belts, hoses and leaks

Signs you need it

  • Your oil-change reminder or sticker is due
  • You are reaching a mileage milestone
  • Low or dark oil on the dipstick
  • It has been a while since the last visit

What to expect: Bring your mileage and any service records you have, and mention any trip coming up. Scheduling maintenance ahead of a road trip is always a good idea.

Virginia State Inspection

Virginia requires a safety inspection every year for most registered vehicles. Sixty Service handles Virginia State Inspections right here in Richmond, VA 23236.

What gets checked

  • Brakes and parking brake
  • Headlights, signals and brake lights
  • Steering, suspension, tires and wheels
  • Horn, wipers, mirrors and glass

When to come in

Service No. 06

Tire Rotation

Front and rear tires wear differently. Rotating them on a regular schedule evens out that wear, so the set lasts longer and the car handles consistently.

What it covers

  • Tire rotation
  • Tread and tire condition check
  • Tire pressure set to spec

Signs you need it

  • Tread wearing faster on one axle
  • It has been several oil changes since the last rotation
  • New vibration or noise from the tires

What to expect: Tire rotation pairs well with oil service. Ask about it when you schedule maintenance.

Service No. 07

Engine & Throttle-Body Repair

Carbon builds up on throttle bodies and in intakes over time. The result can be a rough or surging idle, hesitation or stalling. Engine repair here ranges from throttle-body service to intake and sensor work.

What it covers

  • Throttle-body cleaning and repair
  • Intake manifold work
  • Engine sensors and idle problems
  • Leaks and gaskets

Signs you need it

  • Idle is rough, high or surging
  • Hesitation when you press the gas
  • Engine stalls at stops
  • Check-engine light with drivability issues

What to expect: Engine repair starts with a diagnosis, so you understand what was found and what the repair involves before the work gets underway.

Service questions

Inspections, alignments and more

What does a Virginia State Inspection check?

The annual Virginia safety inspection covers items such as brakes, lights, steering and suspension, tires and wheels, horn, wipers, mirrors, windshield and glass, and the exhaust system.

How often do I need a Virginia inspection?

Most vehicles registered in Virginia need a safety inspection every 12 months. The sticker on your windshield shows the month it expires.

How do I know if I need an alignment?

Common signs are the car pulling to one side, a steering wheel that sits off-center when driving straight, and tires wearing unevenly on one edge. Hitting a big pothole or curb is another good reason to check.

Can you tell me what is wrong before fixing it?
