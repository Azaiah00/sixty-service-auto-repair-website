Our story

The kind of shop you tell your neighbors about

Some shops treat a car like a ticket number. Sixty Service treats it like it belongs to a neighbor, because it usually does.

Sixty Service, Inc. is a family owned and operated auto repair shop at 11013 Research Ct in Richmond, VA 23236, in the North Chesterfield area. It has been fixing cars for more than twenty years and has been on file with the Better Business Bureau since 2007.

Walk into the bays and you will see a bit of everything on the lifts: daily drivers, family SUVs and the occasional classic convertible. The work is the same either way. Find the real problem, fix it properly and explain it clearly.

That approach shows up in the reviews. Sixty Service holds a 4.9 rating from 72 Google reviews and a 5.0 rating from 170 reviews on SureCritic, where one customer titled their review simply “An Honest Family Run Business.”

What we stand for
